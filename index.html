About

I design interfaces that hold up at every size — and in the hand.

I'm a product and interface designer working across mobile, tablet and web. Most of my work lives in the seam between those three: the same product, rethought for a 6.1-inch phone, an 11-inch tablet and a 27-inch display — without losing the idea that made it good in the first place.

I care about the unglamorous parts. Type scales that survive translation. Touch targets that survive gloves. Focus rings that survive a redesign. The 3D device above is a small argument for the same thing: the presentation only works because the geometry underneath it is actually built, not faked with a flat image.
